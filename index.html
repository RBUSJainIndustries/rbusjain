About R.B. Umrao Singh Jain Industries

Established in 1992, R. B. Umrao Singh Jain Industries is a metal perforated sheet manufacturer based in Rewari, Haryana, India. We manufacture and supply a wide range of perforated metal products and industrial filtration screens for customers across India.

Our product range includes stainless steel perforated sheets, SS vacuum filter screens, centrifugal filter screens for sugar industries, supporting liners (Dovex Type) for centrifugal machines, Rice Polishing Jali and industrial metal screens.

Our perforated sheets are manufactured using materials such as Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Mild Steel, Galvanized Iron, Aluminium, Brass and Copper. Depending on the application, perforations can be supplied in round, square, triangle, oblong and oval patterns.

With technical experience, modern manufacturing facilities and quality-focused production, we serve industries including sugar, food processing, pharmaceuticals, paper, cement, chemical, automobile, fertilizer, power and other industrial sectors.

Our objective is to provide reliable, application-specific metal screens with consistent perforation, durability and cost-effective performance.
