About R. B. Umrao Singh Jain Industries

Our products are known for their high tensile strength, durable finish, precision perforation and quality materials. We manufacture products using materials including Stainless Steel, Carbon Steel, Mild Steel, Galvanized Iron (GI), Aluminium, Brass and Copper.

These products find applications in food processing, sugar mills, pharmaceuticals, cement plants, automobiles, chemical industries, fertilizer plants, paper mills, power projects, textile industries and construction.

Applications of Metal Perforated Sheets

We manufacture Steel Perforated Sheets, Stainless Steel Perforated Sheets, Metal Jali and Industrial Filtration Screens designed for demanding industrial applications. Our products provide reliable performance, durable surface finish, uniform perforation and high tensile strength.

Our products are extensively used across the following industries:

  • Food & Food Processing Units – drying, sorting and filtering
  • Paper & Board Mills – filtration and drying applications
  • Sugar Mills – screening, crystallization and juice filtration
  • Automobile Industry – exhaust components, grills and protective panels
  • Pharmaceutical Industry – ventilation, partitions and tablet sieving
  • Power Projects – support structures and protective covers
  • Fertilizer Plants – granulation, screening and material handling
  • Cement Industries – dust separation and reinforcement
  • Chemical Industries – filtration and containment
  • Construction Sector – cladding, safety grills and facades
  • Petrochemical Industry – refining and containment screens
  • Textile Mills – drying and filtration
  • Architectural Applications – facades, sunshades and false ceilings
  • Household Applications – kitchen filters, ventilators and protective mesh
