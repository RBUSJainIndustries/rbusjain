Perforated Sheet Manufacturer in Haryana, India

R. B. Umrao Singh Jain Industries is a perforated sheet manufacturer in Rewari, Haryana, supplying metal perforated sheets and industrial screens to B2B customers across India. Our product range includes stainless steel perforated sheet, SS 304 perforated sheet, SS 316 perforated sheet, carbon steel perforated sheet, galvanized iron perforated sheet, aluminium perforated sheet, brass perforated metal sheet and copper perforated sheet.

We also manufacture specialized products including stainless steel vacuum filter screens, centrifugal filter screens for sugar industries, sugar industry filtration screens, Rice Polishing Jali, rice mill spare parts jali, supporting liners and industrial filtration metal sheets. Perforation patterns include round hole, square hole, triangle hole, oblong hole and oval hole, depending on the product specification and application.

Our products are suitable for filtration, screening, separation, drying, ventilation, protection and architectural applications in industries such as sugar, food processing, pharmaceuticals, cement, chemicals, paper, rice milling, fertilizers and automobile manufacturing.
